# 2026-08-10 — VLAN 50 (IoT), SolarEdge inverter moved onto it

## Why

The SolarEdge inverter re-answers the mDNS service-discovery meta-query
(`_services._dns-sd._udp.local`) roughly every 7 seconds with a TTL of
**60** instead of the standard 4500. Those short-TTL answers overwrite
the shared cache entries for every service type they mention, so
browsing clients on VLAN 10 expire their entire service list on the
inverter's clock and relearn it seconds later.

Measured from a wireless VLAN 10 client (a Mac, held awake with
`caffeinate -i` so its own dark-wake naps did not pollute the data):

| Window | Adds | Removals | Mass-expiry bursts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10h overnight baseline | ~700/hour | ~700/hour | one every ~179s, flat all night |
| 8 min after the move | 31 | **0** | none |

The overnight run is what ruled out "it is still settling after the
switch resets" — a settling network decays, and this did not: the rate
was identical at 02:00 with the house asleep as at 20:00.

Ruled out along the way: the RB5009 mDNS repeater (A/B tested off for 11
minutes, no effect — restored), the Unleashed Bonjour Gateway
(confirmed disabled), IPv6 RA churn from Thread border routers (router
set stable across flush events), and the VLAN 10 querier gap fixed the
previous day (`changes/2026-08-09-vlan10-querier-restore.md`) — that was
a real and separate fault. The inverter had been on VLAN 10 for a long
time; the resets are what prompted looking closely enough to see it.

## What changed

A new IoT segment, VLAN 50 / `10.1.50.0/24`, IPv4-only, built across all
ten devices. Full design in `topology/vlan50-iot.md`.

**office-rb5009** — `vlan50` interface on the bridge, `10.1.50.1/24`,
bridge + `sfp-sfpplus1` tagged for VLAN 50, `vlan50-iot` pool
(`.20-.254`), `dhcp-vlan50` (8h leases, ctrld at 10.1.30.20 for DNS,
the four internal NTP servers), a reservation putting the inverter at
`10.1.50.10`, and a `solaredge.internal.greyrock.io` record.
`vlan50` joined the `LAN` and `DNS-FORCE` interface lists.

**Spine (three CRS309s)** — VLAN 50 tagged on the used SFP+ ports, no
bridge membership, identical in shape to VLANs 10/20/4000: office and
game-room on `sfp-sfpplus1-4`, garage on `sfp-sfpplus1-3`.

**Access (six ICX)** — `vlan 50 name IoT by port` with the uplink
tagged (`1/2/2` on the 8200s, `1/3/2` on the 7150s). AP ports are not
members.

**game-room-icx-7150** — port `1/1/1` (`SolarEdge`) moved from VLAN 10
untagged to VLAN 50 untagged. FastIron allows untagged membership in one
VLAN only, so the port left VLAN 10 first; port `1/1/2` (Zigbee) stayed
behind. Port-level settings (`stp-bpdu-guard`, `broadcast limit 500`)
are VLAN-independent and untouched.

## Policy decisions

- **No isolation rules.** VLAN 50 routes like VLANs 10 and 20. Internet
  works through the existing defconf masquerade
  (`out-interface-list=WAN`) with no NAT change.
- **`LAN` membership is mandatory**, not cosmetic: the input chain ends
  in `drop all not coming from LAN`, so DHCP and DNS would go
  unanswered without it.
- **Excluded from `mdns-repeat-ifaces`**, which stays
  `vlan10,vlan20,bridge`. This is the entire point of the segment.
- **IPv4 only** — no ULA, no PD carve, no address on the interface.
- Guest isolation already covers the new segment: the rule dropping
  VLAN 4000 to the `internal` address-list matches `10.1.0.0/16`.
- Home Assistant needed no change; it has no SolarEdge integration.

## Verified

- `10.1.50.10` reachable from a VLAN 10 client at `ttl=63` — one router
  hop, confirming it is genuinely off VLAN 10 — and
  `solaredge.internal.greyrock.io` resolves to it.
- `show vlan 50` on all six ICX: VLAN 50 named IoT with the expected
  uplink tagged; `show vlan 10` on game-room-icx-7150 no longer lists
  1/1/1 and still lists 1/1/2.
- `/interface bridge vlan print` on all three CRS309s: VLAN 50 row with
  the expected `CURRENT-TAGGED` ports and no `bridge`.
- The mDNS measurement in the table above.

## Notes for the next reader

- Adding a wireless IoT SSID later means tagging VLAN 50 on the ICX AP
  ports at that time — it is deliberately absent from them now.
- VLAN 20 is unqueried by choice, not by oversight, and that was
  re-confirmed on 2026-08-10. Nothing on it depends on reliable
  multicast: NTP and the k8s BGP session are unicast, the NAS advertises
  over mDNS then transfers by unicast, the KVMs are unicast. The
  CRS309s snoop it bridge-wide and simply age out to flooding, which
  `flood-unregistered` makes harmless at this traffic volume. Adding a
  querier means also enabling snooping on the ICX that hold VLAN 20
  hosts — config that must be re-applied after any factory reset — to
  buy selective forwarding of a trickle of mDNS. Not worth it until a
  multicast-dependent workload lands on VLAN 20; then it is one command
  on office-icx-8200 plus `passive` on whichever ICX has the hosts.
- These captures are the first since the mgmt-IP renumber, so the
  committed configs now match the live per-room addressing.
- RoMON, the back-to-home WireGuard tunnel, and `/ip cloud` are absent
  from the RB5009 by choice, as is any `/ipv6 nd` override (there is no
  IPv6 DNS server on this network). Their absence in these captures is
  intentional, not lost config.
